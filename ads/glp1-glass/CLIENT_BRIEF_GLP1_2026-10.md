---

Please use these guidelines for all new MyFastRx GLP-1 videos. Our goal is clean, professional, trustworthy advertising
that makes the offer easy to understand and encourages viewers to check eligibility.

## 1. Creative style
- Primary audience: women ages 35–64.
- Use relatable adults, natural expressions, and realistic lifestyle scenes.
- Preferred female lead: approximately mid-40s, shoulder-length dark brown hair, average or mid-size build.
- Keep the design modern, uncluttered, and consistent with our brand.
- Avoid exaggerated reactions, overly glamorous models, and miracle weight-loss messaging.
- Give each video one main idea. Do not squeeze every selling point into every ad.

## 2. Video structure and delivery
- Target length: **15–20 seconds**, unless we request otherwise.
- Primary format: **1080 × 1920 vertical, 9:16**.
- Provide a **1080 × 1350 vertical, 4:5** feed version when requested.
- Square versions are optional and only needed when requested.
- Adapt layouts for each format rather than simply cropping.
- Deliver the finished MP4 and editable source files.

Use readable captions so the video works without sound. Keep voiceover clear and music subtle. Use licensed assets and
music cleared for paid advertising.

Keep important text, prices, logos, and disclaimers away from placement controls and screen edges. Check readability on
a phone before delivery.

## 3. Logo and product imagery
- Use the official logo we provide. Do not recreate or alter it.
- Use the approved logo with italic "Fast," red "Fast" and pulse, and dark navy "My" and "Rx," unless we supply another version.
- **Never place the MyFastRx logo on medication vials.** MyFastRx does not manufacture medication.
- Use realistic packaging, glass, labels, and cold packs.
- Get our approval before using AI-generated medication or packaging imagery.
- Do not invent pharmacy names, medication labels, certifications, or package contents.
- Never show needles, syringes, or someone injecting medication.
- Never use Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, or Zepbound pens or packaging to represent our compounded medication.
- Do not show a fixed number of bottles unless we approve it. Packaging and shipment schedules can vary by pharmacy and plan.

## 4. GLP-1 pricing
These are our established prices. Confirm the active promotion with us before producing each video.

| Plan | Compounded Semaglutide | Compounded Tirzepatide |
|---|---:|---:|
| First-month promotional price | **$69** | **$129** |
| Regular 1-month price | $139 | $199 |
| 3-month plan | $297 total / $99 per month equivalent | $498 total / $166 per month equivalent |
| 6-month plan | $534 total / $89 per month equivalent | $894 total / $149 per month equivalent |
| 12-month plan | $948 total / $79 per month equivalent | $1,488 total / $124 per month equivalent |

Pricing rules:
- Preferred offer wording: **"Compounded Semaglutide starting at $69"** or **"Compounded Tirzepatide starting at $129."**
- Clearly identify the medication as compounded in the main on-screen copy, not only in the disclaimer.
- Include a readable **"Introductory offer"** qualifier near the promotional price.
- The $70 promotional savings are applied automatically. Do not show MY70 or tell viewers to enter a code.
- Do not imply the introductory prices continue indefinitely.
- **Never say "$69, then $79/month" or "$129, then $124/month."** The $79 and $124 figures are monthly equivalents of
  prepaid 12-month plans, not regular monthly refill prices.
- For short promotional videos, focus on the introductory offer. Include multi-month pricing only when requested.
- If showing a monthly equivalent, clearly show the plan length and total prepaid amount.
- Do not invent countdowns, expiration dates, limited quantities, or savings claims.

## 5. Approved selling points
Choose a few that support the video's main idea:
- "Provider-guided GLP-1 weight-loss care."
- "Start online."
- "Online intake and licensed provider review."
- "Medication and shipping included."
- "Same price at any provider-approved dose."
- "No subscription or membership fees."
- "U.S.-licensed providers."
- "Dispensed by U.S. pharmacies."
- "Cold 2-day shipping," when confirmed for the advertised service.
- "Prescription required. Subject to provider approval."

Pricing includes provider review, prescribed medication, and shipping. Include "supplies" only when we confirm they are included.

The basic process is: **Online intake → licensed provider review → pharmacy fulfillment and delivery if prescribed.**

## 6. Claims and wording we must avoid
- Never promise approval, a prescription, a particular dose, or a specific result.
- Never promise a certain number of pounds lost or results within a fixed time.
- Avoid "guaranteed weight loss," "risk-free," "no side effects," "miracle," or similar claims.
- Never describe compounded medication as FDA-approved.
- Never claim it is identical to, a generic version of, or proven equivalent to a brand-name medication.
- Do not advertise our GLP-1 program as diabetes treatment.
- Do not give dosing instructions or tell viewers which medication they should take.
- Do not imply viewers can select any dose without provider review.
- Do not promise delivery within two days of purchase. Two-day shipping begins after pharmacy fulfillment.
- Do not promise immediate or guaranteed same-day provider approval.
- Do not say every order includes a live doctor video call. Use "licensed provider review."
- Do not advertise insurance coverage. We do not accept insurance.
- Do not make clinical benefit claims or medication comparisons unless we provide approved wording.

## 7. Body imagery and testimonials
- Never use before-and-after photos, transformation sequences, body close-ups, pinched fat, scales showing dramatic
  losses, or measuring-tape shots.
- Avoid body shaming or wording that assumes the viewer's weight or medical condition, such as "Are you overweight?" or
  "Struggling with obesity?"
- Focus on the service and offer, such as **"Compounded GLP-1 care starting at $69."**
- Never create fake patient testimonials or present an actor or AI character as an actual patient.
- Use real testimonials only when we provide approved wording and permission.
- Do not imply a model's appearance is the result of our treatment.
- Do not show real patient information in portal screenshots, intake forms, labels, or other visuals.

## 9. Required disclaimers
For videos showing only the introductory $69 or $129 offer:

> "Introductory pricing shown. Regular pricing applies to future orders. MyFastRx does not manufacture medications;
> product appearance and labeling may vary. Compounded medication. Not FDA-approved. Results may vary."

For videos showing multi-month pricing:

> "Pricing reflects promotional or multi-month plans. MyFastRx does not manufacture medications; product appearance and
> labeling may vary. Compounded medication. Not FDA-approved. Results may vary."

Also include:

> "Prescription required. Subject to provider approval."

Disclaimers must be readable on mobile, with sufficient contrast and time on screen. Design space for them from the
beginning. A disclaimer does not fix a misleading headline or visual.

## 10. Call to action and destination
- Preferred CTA: **"Check if I qualify."**
- Display URL: **MyFastRx.com**
- Landing page: **https://www.myfastrx.com/weight-loss/special/**
- Do not imply clicking the CTA guarantees treatment or a prescription.

## 11. Basic concepts for new videos
1. Semaglutide $69 offer: lead with the introductory price, show what's included, and close with the qualification CTA.
2. Tirzepatide $129 offer: same approach, focused on compounded Tirzepatide.
3. Three simple steps: online intake, provider review, and delivery if prescribed.
4. What's included: provider review, prescribed medication, and shipping.
5. No membership fees: focus on no subscription or membership fees, followed by the offer.
6. Same price at any approved dose: emphasize flat pricing while keeping provider approval clear.
7. Care from home: show a relatable adult completing an intake on a phone or laptop.
8. Trust first: lead with U.S.-licensed providers, U.S. pharmacies, and approved credibility assets.
9. Clear pricing: present the offer and included services with a clean, simple layout.
10. FAST start online: use quick transitions through the online process without promising immediate approval or delivery.

**Start with the Semaglutide offer, Tirzepatide offer, and three-step process concepts.**
