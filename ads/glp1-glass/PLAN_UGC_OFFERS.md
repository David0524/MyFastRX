Rules: CLIENT_BRIEF_GLP1_2026-10.md (guidance) + HANDOFF.md decisions, UGC_PLAYBOOK.md. She is always a presenter or a
prospective customer, never a patient with results.

## What makes it UGC, not an ad with a person in it
1. **A format people already recognize in their feed:** the receipt, the fine-print read, the "do it with me". Familiar
   formats get watched before the brain files them as "ad".
2. **A character, not a spokesperson:** a name, a life, a dry sense of humour, the same face every time.
3. **A visual payoff the viewer waits for:** a receipt printing line by line, the fine print zooming in, a step counter
   ticking 1-2-3. Open loop at 0 s, payoff by 12-15 s.
4. **Constant movement:** a cut or new visual every 1.5-2.5 s; at least one change of place; props in her hands.
5. **Sound that feels native:** her voice up front, small real sounds (receipt printer, pen click, car door), no ad music
   under the talk.
6. **One idea, landed hard,** then the offer and "Check if I qualify".

## The host: "Dana" (character bible)
- 46, Black (or Afro-Latina), shoulder-length dark brown hair (a soft twist-out), average / mid-size build, small gold
  hoops, reading glasses pushed up on her head. Relatable, warm, dry humour; underreacts rather than overreacts.
- Life: office manager, two teenagers, busy, practical, does not like being sold to. Allergic to subscriptions and fine print.
- Films herself on her phone: phone propped on the fridge door, the car dash, a mug on the coffee table. Not aesthetic.
  Slightly awkward on purpose.

  navy zip hoodie.
- Voice: her first approved take's voice, then a locked prompt (only the words change).

## Edit language (all three)
- Native TikTok captions (white, black outline), 2-4 words, one sticker-style text hook in the first 2 s.
- Cuts every 1.5-2.5 s; a 5-8% punch-in on the punchline line ("...None." / "Subscription... nope.").
- Small real sounds only: receipt printer, pen click, car door, mug on the table. Music only on the end card, licensed.
- The coded pieces (receipt, magnifier zooms of real site copy, 1-2-3 stickers, end card) are built in code: exact
  wording, free, and the price line always carries "Compounded" + "Introductory offer".
- Every video: 2-3 hooks re-cut from the same takes, so 4 videos -> 10-12 ads.
