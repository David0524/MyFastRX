- **Line 2:** plain, no punch on the price. (The qualification is on screen; it is not read.)

- **Line 4:** steady and matter-of-fact.
- **Line 5:** the proof point. Slow down slightly; land "doesn't increase" with calm certainty.
- **Line 6:** friendly and unhurried, "dot com" clear.
