# ElevenLabs VO: the 10 s "No strings" spot (v8)

Same voice as the 28.8 s film (Susan Kathleen) and the same settings, so the two spots sound like one campaign.
Export WAV, or the highest quality your plan allows.

The scratch cut uses lines from the earlier reads. This is the dedicated 10 s read. It runs tighter, so the
disclaimer gets a longer hold on the end card.

Please record **3 takes in one file**, with about a second of silence between them. I pick one take and cut only in
its pauses.

## Settings (Multilingual v2)
- Stability 55–60%, Similarity 75–80%, Style 0–10%, Speaker boost on.
- **Speed 1.05.** The read has to fit in 10 s; please don't go faster than 1.1.

## Type it exactly like this

```
G-L-P-1 care, without the strings.

No membership fees. No automatic refills.

Starting at sixty-nine dollars.

My Fast R X dot com.
```

## Direction
- **Line 1:** the hook. Easy and assured, with a small smile on "without the strings." Start clean, with no breath
  before "G". Keep "G-L-P-1" tight and even (like take 4 of the GLP-1 takes).
- **Line 2:** two short, even statements with a real full stop after "fees." On screen, each one lands as a string
  falls off the vial, so keep them crisp and unhurried.
- **Line 3:** plain, with no punch on the price. The qualification is on screen; it isn't read.
- **Line 4:** friendly sign-off. Say "dot com" clearly.

Target: about 7.5 s of speech in total (line 1 ~2.5 s, line 2 ~2.4 s, line 3 ~1.5 s, line 4 ~1.2 s).
