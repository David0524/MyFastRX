# Video 5: autumn UGC, research + plan (Oct 11, 2026). Nothing generated yet.

## Research (first-party where possible; vendor-blog numbers marked "directional")
**TikTok Creative Codes (TikTok Marketing Science, first-party)**
- 90% of ad recall and 80% of awareness impact is captured in the first 6 s ("Value of View", 2020). Land the value proposition early.
- "TikTok-first" ads (lo-fi, 9:16, real people, platform lingo): ~3.3x more actions (clicks/shares) than non-native ads (Marketcast, 2022);
  moving TV-style assets to TikTok-first: +37% purchase intent, +38% favourability, 2x engagement/watch time (Kantar, 3,500 ads, 2021).
- Hook types (Lumen, US SMB, 2021): suspense +16% watch time; surprise 1.7x view-through; emotion 1.7x awareness.
- Structure: hook (3-6 s) -> body (product + message) -> close (clear CTA). Attention spikes at each scene change
  (Neuro-Insight, 2022); faster scene changes "increase chances of making an impression".
- Sound: 73% would "stop and look" at a sound-on ad; users 15% more likely to skip an ad without sound; sound-on gives a 55% lift
  in communicating new information (Kantar/Hotspex, 2021).
- 50% of campaign success is creative quality (Nielsen Catalina). 77% of users like brands using platform trends (Flamingo, 2021).

**Meta Reels (Meta x Toluna, 100 Reels ads x 65 creative variables, 4 markets, 2024; "likelihood of a top-20% ad")**
- Brand + main message in the first 5 s: 1.7x purchase intent. Visual + audio hook in the first 1-2 s: 1.5x.
- Slice-of-life, everyday-people scenes: 1.5x purchase intent. Speech + music together: 2.0x brand interest.
- Direct response: product + context (USPs/details) 5.3x; product shown more than once 2.7x; brand presence capped at ~25% of the
  runtime 4.8x; CTA in visual and/or audio 1.9x; emojis 2.5x.

**UGC vs studio** (agency-published, directional): UGCera A/B across 84 paid-social campaigns (2024-25): +21% conversion rate,
-17% CPA for UGC creative.

**Category + season**
- KFF Health Tracking Poll (Oct 27-Nov 2, 2025, n=1,350): 12% of US adults currently use a GLP-1; 56% of users say it was
  difficult to afford. Cost is the barrier -> price-led pitch is right.
- Google Trends (Cambridge, 2024): "weight loss"/"diet" searches peak every January and bottom out in December.
- NEJM 2000 (NIH, n=195): average holiday gain is small (0.37 kg / ~0.8 lb) but isn't reversed afterwards; 14% gained >5 lb;
  people *believe* they gain 5+ lb. The "I'll start in January" mindset is real and widely shared.
- Ad costs: Meta price-per-ad +12% YoY in H1 2026 (reported); vendors expect Q4 CPMs to climb from late October and peak
  around Black Friday (directional: +15-100%). Launching in mid-October is the cheaper window; the creative has to work hard.
- Compliance climate: 30+ state AGs wrote to Meta (Dec 2025) about GLP-1 ads with exaggerated results, AI fake testimonials and
  before/after images. Meta bans weight-loss ads that create negative self-perception. Our rules (presenter not patient, no results,
  AI-portrayal label) are exactly what keeps this account safe.

## The pitch
**"Don't wait for January."** Everyone plans to "start after the holidays"; searches only spike in January. The ad gives people
permission to start the conversation now, and removes the cost objection (KFF: 56% find GLP-1s hard to afford):
first month $69, no subscription, same price at every dose, provider review online.
Guardrails: never promise results by a date ("lose it before Thanksgiving" is out); "start" = check if you qualify.

## Hook
Spoken + on-screen in the first 1-2 s (Meta 1.5x; TikTok: proposition in the first 3 s):
**"This is your sign to stop waiting for January."** It carries the pitch in the hook itself (the main message in the first 5 s is
1.7x). Test variant (one extra 5 s clip): **"Come with me…"** (slice-of-life 1.5x, but the message lands later).
"I don't know who needs to hear this" is the weakest fit: it is generic and closest to the patronising tone Meta flags.
