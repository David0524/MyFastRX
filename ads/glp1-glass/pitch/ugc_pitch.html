MyFastRx.com review

The site already makes the promise. It just hides it.

Ads will send people to the site as it is today, so the ads have to match what they find there word for word. Here is what we saw, and the small fixes that would lift every ad we run.

Keep

The offer page leads with GLP-1 Weight Loss Starting at $69, says Provider review, medication, and shipping included, and lists no membership fees, no insurance required and cancel anytime.

This is our message. Every ad repeats these exact phrases so the landing page feels like the ad continued.

Fix

The homepage hero says Healthcare, Simplified. Delivered to Your Door. and its main button reads Explore Treatment Options. Nothing above the fold says what makes MyFastRx different.

Point all paid traffic to /weight-loss/special/, whose button already says See if I qualify. Longer term, make the homepage hero "GLP-1 care, without the strings." with "No membership fees · No automatic refills" under it.

Fix

"No automatic refills" is true but is never stated on the GLP-1 pages. The NAD+ page says No automatic billing.

Add one line near the GLP-1 price: "Nothing ships until you request it." It is the single biggest objection our ads answer.

Fix

The offer page's cancel note says You can pause or cancel your plan at any time before your next refill. That reads like a recurring plan.

Reword it to match how refills actually work. Until then, "No subscription" needs counsel's sign-off before it runs as a headline; "No automatic refills" is the safer claim.

Add

There are no reviews or ratings anywhere, only Thousands of Customers. Trust rests on the LegitScript, BBB and HIPAA badges.

Start collecting real reviews on an independent platform. Until then the ads carry the BBB A rating and "Licensed U.S. providers" as proof. We will not invent testimonials.

Keep

24h Review and Most patients receive their order within a few days of approval, plus free express shipping.

Supporting proof in captions and end cards, never the headline.

The category

The big players went loud. We go plain.

Ro and Hims & Hers spend on celebrities, Super Bowl spots and testimonial volume. MyFastRx can't outspend them and shouldn't copy them. The opening is the frustration neither of them leads with: strings. Memberships, auto-ship, renewals you didn't ask for.

Ro

"Healthier on Ro"

Serena Williams in a Super Bowl debut, framed around health metrics ("vitals, not vanity") rather than looks. Clinical, premium, in-house creative.

Hims & Hers

"Sick of the System"

An anti-establishment tone and a very high volume of Meta ads, much of it testimonial-style UGC. Its GLP-1 Super Bowl ad drew criticism over risk disclosure.

MyFastRx

"Without the strings."

Take the earthy, editorial calm from Ro and the native volume from Hims, and aim it at the frustration both leave open: being billed for something you didn't ask for. "No subscription" is tested as a hook under that line. Full disclosure on every ad.

Testing

Launch wide, then follow the money.

Everything launches at once with even budgets on Meta and TikTok, one campaign per product. We read results in a fixed order: hook first, then host, then format.

Days 1–5

Launch

All 16 videos and 12 statics live, even budgets, AI-content labels on.

Days 5–7

Read

Hook rate (3-second views), hold rate, cost per click to the offer page, cost per started visit.

Week 2

Shift

Budget moves to the top third. The winner is re-cast with the other host to test the host on its own.

Each month

Refresh

Cut the bottom half, write new hooks for the winning concepts, re-shoot the best one as a hero.

Guardrails

Authentic, and on the right side of the line.

Native-looking ads in this category get scrutiny. These rules apply to every frame, and counsel signs off once per format before the first paid run.

Always

  • Hosts explain the service as presenters
  • "Introductory offer. Regular pricing varies by plan." directly beneath $69
  • The approved disclaimer on the end card or in the fine print
  • "See if you qualify", never a promise of a prescription
  • TikTok and Meta AI-generated content labels switched on
  • The client's vial file, label exact, in the brand's own setting

Never

  • "I've been on it", results, pounds, before and after
  • Needles, syringes, scales or body close-ups
  • A person holding or taking the medication
  • "Everyone qualifies" or "guaranteed"
  • Competitors, other brands or "unlike others"
  • Invented reviews, ratings or customers
