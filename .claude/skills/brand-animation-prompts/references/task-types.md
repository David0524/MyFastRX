# Task types

Paste the matching goal into WHAT IT NEEDS TO DO, then fill its slots.

**Intro / show opener / channel bumper.** "Feel like the opening of a show. Build energy from the first frame, move constantly, and land on the logo like a payoff. Think title sequence, not short story. A viewer should feel it's starting by second three."

**Ad / promo.** "Make {{product or offer}} the hero. Show the benefit to {{audience}}, not a list of features. Build to the call to action: {{CTA}}. It should make the viewer want {{outcome}}."

**Explainer.** "The viewer must understand this: {{content}}. Story spine, in order: {{3–5 ideas, not timed beats}}. End on the takeaway line: {{line}}." Every claim must come from the verified brief.

**Product demo.** "Show {{product}} solving {{problem}} in a way that's instantly clear with no sound. Spine: the problem, the product, the result. End on {{CTA}}."

**Announcement / launch / event.** "Make {{news}} feel like an event. Lead with the reveal, then {{date, place, or how to get it}}, legible and held long enough to read."

**Sign-off / outro / end card.** "Wind down with a satisfying beat and land on the logo and {{CTA or subscribe line}}. Calm enough to read everything."

**Anything else.** Write a one-paragraph goal in the same shape: what it must feel like, what the viewer must understand or do, and how it ends.

## Render problems

Replace only the lines causing the issue.

- **Doesn't feel like its task type** (for example, an intro that plays like a story): the prompt is too specific. Cut beats and restate the goal paragraph, with creative freedom.
- **Too chaotic:** add "Pick one visual idea and commit to it."
- **Too static:** strengthen the motion lines: camera always moving, background always moving, prop-driven transitions.
- **Copied a reference image's layout:** add the "reference only, not a frame" line for that image.
- **Character drifts off-model:** attach more poses and repeat "keep them consistent in every frame."
- **Wrong vibe:** revisit the style choice in styles.md, rather than adding adjectives.
- **Text cut off on phone:** add or tighten the safe-zone line.
- **Logo altered:** restate the logo rule and confirm the official file is attached and numbered correctly.
